# aiaiaiai Product Identity and Trademark Policy

**Status:** initial policy; it does not claim that every listed mark is registered in every jurisdiction.

The Apache-2.0 source license does not grant permission to present a derivative product as official aiaiaiai software.

## Protected Identity

The project reserves control over identity associated with `aiaiaiai`, `aiaiaiai.org`, official aiaiaiai logos, emblems, wordmarks, and visual identity, and confusingly similar names or presentation that imply official status, sponsorship, endorsement, or canonical origin.

## Allowed Referential Use

Truthful referential statements are allowed where applicable law permits them. A third party may state that software uses, derives from, or interoperates with this project, provided the statement does not imply an official relationship that does not exist.

## Forks and Derivatives

A fork or derivative permitted by Apache-2.0 must use its own product identity unless the project steward grants explicit permission otherwise. It must not reuse official visual identity in a way that makes users reasonably believe they are using canonical aiaiaiai software.

## Permissions

The project may grant written permission for integrations, distributions, research, events, partnerships, or other uses. Permission may be scoped and may be revised according to its terms.
